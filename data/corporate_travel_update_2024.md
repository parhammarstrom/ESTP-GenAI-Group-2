# Corporate Travel Policy Update — Q4 2024 (Supplementary)

This document provides updated guidance reflecting recent changes to the company travel program. Where this document and earlier policy pages differ, the figures and rules in this update should be treated as the current reference.

## Hotel Allowances (Revised)

Following the 2024 cost review, the standard hotel cap has been adjusted. The new maximum hotel rate is €220 per night, inclusive of all taxes and service charges. This supersedes the previously published limit of €180 per night. Manager approval is no longer required for the Central Paris Hotel, as it now falls within the updated cap.

For London, the negotiated rate for Westminster Business Hotel has been renegotiated to €195 per night. The London City Hotel now includes complimentary breakfast at a revised rate of €168 per night.

## Meal Allowance (Revised)

The maximum meal allowance has been increased to €75 per day, effective 1 October 2024. This covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner combined. This update replaces the earlier figure of €50 per day.

Tips at restaurants are now reimbursable without a percentage cap, provided the total tip does not exceed 15% of the meal cost. This is a relaxation of the earlier 10% cap.

## Flight Class Rules (Revised)

The threshold for booking business class has been lowered. Flights of five hours or more may now be booked in business class. Previously, the threshold was six hours.

Premium economy may be selected for any flight longer than three hours when its fare is no more than €200 above the lowest reasonable economy fare. Manager approval is no longer required for premium economy bookings, as long as the fare difference is within the stated limit.

The fare difference cap for choosing a nonstop over a connecting flight has been raised to €150. This supersedes the earlier €100 limit.

## Baggage Policy (Revised)

The company now reimburses up to two standard checked bags per employee per trip. Previously, only one standard checked bag was covered. This change applies to all trips approved on or after 1 November 2024.

Additional baggage for personal items is now also reimbursable up to a total of €100 per trip.

## Expense Claim Deadline (Revised)

The deadline for submitting expense claims has been extended from 30 days to 45 days after returning from a business trip. This gives employees additional time to collect and submit receipts.

## Transport (Revised)

First-class train travel is now permitted for journeys of more than two hours, provided the cost difference from standard class does not exceed €20. For journeys of two hours or less, standard class remains the default.

Taxi fares are reimbursable without a per-ride cap, provided the journey is business-related and a receipt is submitted.

## Paris Hotels (Revised)

The negotiated rate for Seine Business Hotel (La Défense) has been updated to €190 per night. The Central Paris Hotel rate remains at €185 per night, but as noted above, no manager approval is required because the new cap is €220.

A new hotel has been added to the Paris portfolio:

Grand Arc de Triomphe Hotel
€210 per night. Located near the Arc de Triomphe in the 16th arrondissement. Includes Wi-Fi, breakfast, and access to a rooftop bar.

## General Note

Some earlier policy documents may still reference the previous figures. Employees should use this update as the authoritative reference for the items listed above. If there is any ambiguity, the employee should contact the Travel & Expense team before booking or claiming.
